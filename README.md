## Official & Planning

Essential official government portals, tourist boards, entry guidelines, and cultural heritage booking systems.

- [Italia.it (Official Tourism Portal)](https://www.italia.it/en) - Official national travel guide featuring regional destination highlights, itineraries, and cultural event calendars.
- [Ministero degli Affari Esteri - Visas](https://vistoperitalia.esteri.it/home/en) - Official consular portal for checking Schengen visa entry rules and passport requirements for foreign visitors.
- [Ministero della Cultura (MiC)](https://cultura.gov.it/) - Official government ministry overseeing national museums, archaeological parks, and historic heritage sites.
- [Aeronautica Militare Weather (MeteoAM)](https://www.meteoam.it/) - Official national meteorological service providing regional weather forecasts, wind warnings, and climate tracking.

## Transportation

Navigating high-speed rail networks, regional trains, public transit, and domestic aviation.

- [Trenitalia (Frecce & Regional Trains)](https://www.trenitalia.com/en.html) - National railway operator offering high-speed Frecciarossa services and regional train connections across Italy.
- [Italo Treno](https://www.italotreno.com/en) - Private high-speed rail network connecting major Italian cities with modern passenger trains.
- [Atac Roma / Trenitalia Urban Transit](https://www.atac.roma.it/) - Official public transit guides and ticketing portals for managing urban metro, tram, and bus networks.
- [ITA Airways](https://www.ita-airways.com/) - National flag carrier operating international long-haul flights and domestic routes through Rome (FCO) and Milan (MXP).

## Destinations & Regions

### Rome & Lazio

- [Turismo Roma (Official City Portal)](https://www.turismoroma.it/en) - Official municipal visitor guide for exploring the Colosseum, Vatican museums, historic squares, and neighborhood itineraries.
- [Colosseum Archaeological Park](https://colosseo.it/en/) - Official visitor and ticket reservation portal for the Colosseum, Roman Forum, and Palatine Hill.

### Florence & Tuscany

- [Toscana Promozione Turistica](https://www.visittuscany.com/en/) - Official regional portal for discovering Florence, Siena, Val d'Orcia wine trails, and medieval hilltop towns.
- [Uffizi Galleries Official Portal](https://www.uffizi.it/en) - Official booking and exhibition guide for the Uffizi Gallery, Pitti Palace, and Boboli Gardens in Florence.

### Venice & Veneto

- [Venezia Unica (Official City Portal)](https://www.veneziaunica.it/en) - Official municipal visitor guide for navigating vaporetto water buses, civic museums, and St. Mark's district.

### Milan & Lombardy

- [In-Lombardia (Regional Tourism Portal)](https://www.in-lombardia.it/en) - Official tourist guide for Milan's fashion district, Lake Como, Lake Garda, and Alpine valleys.
- [Duomo di Milano Official Ticket Portal](https://www.duomomilano.it/en/) - Official reservation site for accessing Milan Cathedral rooftop terraces and archaeological areas.

### Naples, Amalfi Coast & South

- [InCampania Tourism Portal](https://www.campania.info/) - Official regional travel guide for Naples, Pompeii archaeological excavations, Sorrento, and the Amalfi Coast.

## Digital Nomad & Remote Work

Resources for long-term travelers and remote professionals taking advantage of Italy's digital nomad visa framework.

- [Italy Digital Nomad Visa (Polizia di Stato / Farnesina)](https://vistoperitalia.esteri.it/) - Official government guidelines detailing income requirements, tax status, and residency application pathways for non-EU remote workers.
- [Talent Garden Milan](https://talentgarden.org/) - Leading European coworking and innovation campus network offering flexible desk space and tech community events.

## Safety & Emergency Services

Practical advice on emergency contacts, tourist police support, and local regulations.

- [112 European Emergency Number](https://www.poliziadistato.it/) - Integrated single European emergency hotline for police (Carabinieri / Polizia), fire (Vigili del Fuoco), and medical assistance.
- [Polizia di Stato Tourist Assistance](https://www.poliziadistato.it/) - Dedicated police division offering multi-language support, traveler safety advice, and lost property reporting.
